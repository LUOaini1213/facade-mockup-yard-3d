Viewer processing (viewer.js, at load time; nothing is baked into these files):
- the sun disc of the partly-cloudy HDRI is clamped (a DirectionalLight at the NOAA sun position is the sun), the sky is
  white-balanced and the lower hemisphere is replaced by the radiance of the lit yard (ground bounce);
- the yard concrete uses the luminance of brushed_concrete with added aggregate specks, damp patches and tyre marks;
- the far backdrop ground is a plane with the clean_asphalt texture, tinted to a weathered grey.
